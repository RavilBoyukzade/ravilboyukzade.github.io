Listings are checked by our moderation team before going live, usually within a few hours.

Tips for a fast sale

  • ✔ Sort and clean the material before listing — it raises both your price and your CO₂ score.
  • ✔ Weigh accurately: listings with precise weight get 2× more offers.
  • ✔ Add at least 3 photos, including one close-up of the material texture.
  • ✔ Reply to offers within a few hours to keep your response rating high.

What happens after you publish

  1. Our team reviews the listing for accuracy and category fit.
  2. It becomes visible to recyclers and buyers who match your filters.
  3. You receive offers you can accept, counter, or decline.
  4. Payment is held in escrow until pickup is confirmed.
