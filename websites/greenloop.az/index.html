HOW IT WORKS

From a spare bag of scrap to a completed, rated deal

1

List your material

Add category, weight, quality grade, photos and your location — listed in minutes.

2

Get offers

Buyers browse, filter and send price offers. Negotiate directly in-app chat.

3

Pay into escrow

Funds are held securely until pickup is confirmed — for both sides' protection.

4

Pickup & delivery

Arrange courier pickup and track the batch until it reaches the buyer.

5

Rate & get paid

Escrow releases automatically, both sides leave a rating, and it's done.

WHY GREENLOOP

Value for every side of the circular economy

🌍

Environmental impact

Reusing materials cuts demand for raw resources and keeps carbon emissions in check — every batch tracked and reported.

💰

Economic savings

Recycled input material costs less than virgin resources, cutting production costs for buyers and creating income for sellers.

🤝

Social wellbeing

Less pollution and lower emissions mean a cleaner environment — and lower long-term healthcare costs for the community.

🔍

Full transparency

Every transaction, pricing rule and seller verification step is recorded and visible — including our published commission policy.

📶

Easy access

Sellers reach buyers across categories instantly; buyers get full material details and can message sellers directly.

⚡

Real efficiency

Automated matching between supply and demand means both sides reach an agreement in a fraction of the usual time.
